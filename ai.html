Connect an AI assistant

Log meals and check your day from Claude, ChatGPT and other assistants.

What it does

Connect eatnibble to an AI assistant and tell it what you ate, e.g. "I had two boiled eggs and a slice of toast for breakfast." The assistant estimates each item's portion, calories, protein, carbs and fat and saves the meal to your eatnibble diary. It appears in the app the next time you open it. You can also ask "What have I eaten today?" or "Show my meals from the last 3 days."

What you need

The eatnibble iPhone app, signed in with Apple, and an assistant that supports MCP connectors, such as Claude or ChatGPT. The server address is:

https://eatnibble-backend.vercel.app/mcp

Connect in ChatGPT

  1. Open Settings → Apps.
  2. Find eatnibble in the directory and select Connect. Until it's listed, turn on Developer mode under Advanced settings and create an app with the server address above.
  3. Sign in when ChatGPT asks to connect eatnibble.
  4. A page opens with a 6-digit code: approve it on your iPhone (below).

Developer mode is available on ChatGPT's paid plans.

Connect in Claude

  1. Open Settings → Connectors.
  2. Find eatnibble in the directory, or choose Add custom connector and paste the server address above.
  3. Select Connect next to eatnibble.
  4. A page opens with a 6-digit code: approve it on your iPhone (below).

Works on Claude on the web, desktop and mobile.

Connect in Gemini

  1. In the Gemini app, open Settings → Connected apps.
  2. Add a custom app and paste the server address above.
  3. Select Connect to eatnibble.
  4. A page opens with a 6-digit code: approve it on your iPhone (below).

Custom apps aren't available on every Gemini plan yet. If you don't see the option, it hasn't reached your account.

Connect in Grok

  1. On grok.com, open Connectors.
  2. Choose New Connector → Custom and paste the server address above.
  3. Select Connect to eatnibble.
  4. A page opens with a 6-digit code: approve it on your iPhone (below).

Custom connectors need a paid Grok plan.

Connect in Cursor

  1. Open Settings → MCP.
  2. Add a new MCP server named eatnibble with the server address above as its URL.
  3. Select Connect or Login next to eatnibble.
  4. A page opens with a 6-digit code: approve it on your iPhone (below).

Cursor opens the connection page in your browser.

Approve the connection on your iPhone

In the eatnibble app, go to Settings → AI Connections → Connect an Assistant and enter the 6-digit code. The app shows which assistant is asking and what it will be able to do. Tap Connect, and the browser page returns you to your assistant. The code expires after 10 minutes.

What the assistant can do

Log a meal (log_meal): adds one meal with its items and estimated calories and macros. It never changes or deletes existing meals; edit or delete them in the app.
Today's summary (get_today_summary): today's meals and the calories and macros eaten so far.
Recent meals (get_recent_meals): meals and daily totals for up to the last 7 days.

Your daily goal isn't shared, so ask with your own target in mind, e.g. "How much protein do I still need to reach 120 g?"

Your data

Your food log stays on your iPhone. While an assistant is connected, the app shares a short summary of the last 7 days of meals (name, time and totals) so the assistant can answer questions, and meals the assistant logs wait on our server only until the app picks them up. Meal photos, your profile, your daily goal and any Apple Health data are never shared. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Disconnect

In the app, go to Settings → AI Connections, swipe the assistant (or open it) and choose Disconnect. When the last assistant is removed, the summary on our server is deleted. Deleting your account removes everything.

Troubleshooting

"Code not valid": codes expire after 10 minutes. Start the connection again in your assistant to get a new one.
A logged meal isn't in the app yet: open the app, or bring it to the foreground, to pull new meals.
The assistant doesn't see a meal I logged in the app: open the app once so it can update the summary.
