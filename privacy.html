Privacy Policy

Last updated: update this date when you publish

eatnibble is a calorie and macro tracker. This page explains, in plain terms, what happens to your data when you use the app.

What we process to analyze a meal

When you take or pick a photo, or type a description of what you ate, that content is sent to our backend, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API for analysis. The photo or text is processed in memory to produce a nutrition estimate (calories, protein, carbs, fat) and is not written to a database or kept after the request completes.

What stays on your device

Your meal log and profile (height, weight, age, activity level, goal) are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us. If you delete the app or clear your log in Settings, that data is gone — we don't keep a copy anywhere.

Third-party services

We use Google's Gemini API to analyze the photo or text you submit. Google's own terms and privacy practices apply to that processing. We don't use any analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs in the app.

Accounts

eatnibble doesn't require an account, sign-in, or email address to use. There is nothing tying your log to an identity on our end.

Children's privacy

eatnibble is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and adjust the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at support@eatnibble.app.
