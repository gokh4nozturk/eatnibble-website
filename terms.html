What eatnibble is

eatnibble is a calorie and macro tracker. You take or pick a photo, or type a description of what you ate; the app sends it to our backend for analysis and gives you an estimate of the calories, protein, carbs and fat, which you can adjust by portion and save to a log kept on your device.

Estimates, not advice

eatnibble does not provide medical, nutritional, dietary or health advice, and using it does not create any professional relationship. The nutrition figures it shows are automated estimates produced by an AI model from a photo or a short text description. They are approximate by nature and can be wrong — portion sizes, hidden ingredients, cooking methods and brand differences all affect the real values.

Use the numbers as a rough guide only. Don't rely on them for decisions where accuracy matters — for example dosing insulin, managing an allergy or a medical condition, or any clinical purpose. Consult a qualified professional for dietary or medical guidance, and seek immediate help for a medical emergency.
