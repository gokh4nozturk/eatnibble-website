Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

How do I log a meal without a photo?

Tap the + button on the Home screen, choose "Describe" and type what you had, e.g. "2 eggs, a slice of toast and orange juice." You'll get the same calorie and macro breakdown as a photo analysis. If you already know the numbers, choose "Enter manually" instead — no analysis needed.

The portion size looks off — can I fix it?

Yes. After analyzing, adjust each item's weight with the −/+ buttons (10 g steps), type an exact amount in grams, or tap ×0.5, ×1, ×1.5 or ×2. The AI's original estimate stays visible as a hint, and calories and macros scale proportionally. You can also edit a saved meal later from History.

Why do I have to sign in?

You can analyze your first 3 meals on a device without an account. After that, eatnibble asks you to sign in with Apple so your daily analysis limit follows your account rather than the device. It's the only sign-in method — no password, and you can use Apple's private relay instead of your real email.

What's the "remaining" number, and where does Apple Health come in?

Remaining = your daily calorie goal, plus the active energy you've burned today (read from Apple Health, if you allow it), minus everything you've logged. If your watch hasn't synced any active energy yet, eatnibble uses a conservative estimate from your walking and running distance until it does. Health access is optional — decline it and the app just uses your goal on its own.

Is my data backed up anywhere?

No — your log, meal photos, water and profile live only on your device and aren't synced to a server. Your account exists only to hold your daily analysis limit; your meals aren't attached to it. Uninstalling the app deletes the local data. To keep a copy, use Profile → Settings → Export data. Photos taken with the in-app camera are also saved to an "eatnibble" album in your Photos library.

How does water tracking work with Apple Health?

Tap a glass on the Home screen's water card for each drink. With Health access, each glass is saved to Apple Health as a water entry, and water logged by other apps is counted too — so your count can't go below what Health already has. Set your daily goal and glass size in Profile.

How do I set up meal or water reminders?

Go to Profile → Settings → Notifications. Meal reminders let you pick your first meal time, how many meals a day and the interval; water reminders run every few hours between a start and end time. If nothing arrives, check that notifications are allowed for eatnibble in iOS Settings.

Where can I see my weekly or monthly averages?

In History, the card at the top shows this week's and this month's daily average. Tap either one for a chart of every day, your goal adherence, highest and lowest day and your most-logged meals. Switch to the calendar view to see each day at a glance. Every history screen has a share button to send a summary as text.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your backend account and its analysis history and can't be undone. "Clear log" in the same screen only wipes your meal entries, meal photos and water history on the device and leaves the account in place.

How do I change the app's language?

eatnibble follows your device's language setting (English or Turkish for now, with English as the fallback). To change it for eatnibble only, go to iPhone Settings → Apps → eatnibble → Language; for the whole phone, Settings → General → Language & Region.

What happens to a photo after it's analyzed?

On our side, it's processed in memory to produce the nutrition estimate and isn't stored afterward. On your phone, it stays attached to the meal in your log until you delete the meal or clear your log. See our Privacy Policy for details.
