Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

How do I log a meal without a photo?

On the Analyze tab, tap "Describe what you ate" and type what you had, e.g. "2 eggs, a slice of toast and orange juice." You'll get the same calorie and macro breakdown as a photo analysis.

The portion size looks off — can I fix it?

Yes. After analyzing, use the stepper next to each item to adjust its weight in grams. Calories and macros scale proportionally as you change it.

Is my data backed up anywhere?

No — your log and profile live only on your device. There's no account and nothing is synced to a server. Uninstalling the app deletes your data.

How do I change the app's language?

eatnibble follows your device's language setting (English or Turkish for now, with English as the fallback). Change it in your iPhone's Settings → General → Language & Region.

What happens to a photo after it's analyzed?

It's processed in memory to produce the nutrition estimate and isn't stored afterward. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Still need help?

Email us at support@eatnibble.app and we'll get back to you.
