Features

Two ways in, one clean log.

No barcode scanning, no searching a food database by hand. Show it or say it.

📷

Photo analysis

Snap a picture of your plate. eatnibble identifies each item and estimates portion size, calories and macros.

💬

Describe it instead

No photo handy? Just type what you ate — "2 eggs, toast, orange juice" — and get the same breakdown.

🎯

Daily goals that fit you

Your calorie and macro targets are calculated from your own height, weight and activity level.

❤️

Apple Health calorie budget

Give read-only access to Apple Health and eatnibble adds the active energy you've burned today onto your target — so "remaining" reflects what you've really got left to eat. Entirely optional.

How it works

Three steps, a few seconds each.

1

Capture or describe

Take a photo, pick one from your library, or just type what you ate.

2

Review the estimate

eatnibble returns calories and macros per item. Adjust portion size and the numbers scale automatically.

3

Save to your log

One tap logs the meal against your daily goal — minus whatever Apple Health says you've burned. Your log stays on your device, and your streak grows.

Your log never leaves your phone.

Meals and profile data are stored locally on your device — signing in doesn't upload them. Photos and descriptions are only sent out to be analyzed, then discarded.
