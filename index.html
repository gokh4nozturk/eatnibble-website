Take a photo

Snap your plate — even from the Lock Screen — or pick a shot from your library. Each item gets its own portion, calories and macros, sized against your plate, the cutlery or, on iPhones with LiDAR, real depth.

Say it or type it

No photo? Tap the mic and say “two eggs, toast and an orange juice”, or type it. Same item-by-item breakdown, with your usual foods suggested as you go.

Scan a barcode

Packaged food? Scan it and the product's own nutrition comes straight from Open Food Facts — no guessing, no daily analysis used.

Read the label

Point the camera at a nutrition table. It's read right on your iPhone — calories, macros, fiber, sugar and sodium.

Scan a menu

Eating out? Photograph the menu and see every dish's estimate, split into what fits today's budget and what doesn't.

Recipes & favorites

Save a recipe once and log a serving in a tap. Star the meals you eat often, re-log frequent ones, or enter the numbers yourself.

Portions

The AI estimates.
You decide.

Every item comes with its own gram estimate. Nudge it by 10 g, type an exact weight, or tap ×0.5, ×1.5 or ×2 — calories and macros rescale instantly.

Something still off? Just tell it — “it was 2 eggs, no rice” — and the estimate is fixed. Drinks are measured in millilitres, and every meal can be edited the same way later.

Apple Health

A calorie budget that moves with you.

Today's steps, workouts and active energy sit right on Home. Meals are saved to Apple Health with their calories and macros, and your water count stays in sync. All of it is optional.

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence it runs entirely on your device. Elsewhere only your logged meals are sent — never Apple Health data.

And more

The small things that keep you going.

Goals that fit you

A calorie target from your body and activity — or your own number — split balanced, high protein, low carb, keto or your own percentages.

Measured maintenance

After a few weeks of meals and weigh-ins, eatnibble measures your real maintenance calories — base your goal on it.

Calorie cycling

Plan low and high days across the week — the weekly total stays the same. Worked out? Make today a high day.

Intermittent fasting

16:8, 18:6, 20:4 or your own plan, with a timer on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

Shared with a partner

Pair with one person and send them a meal you ate together — they add it with a tap. Your logs stay your own.

Evening notes

A short note at the end of the day — low on protein, high on sodium, or right on track — and a weekly summary.

Streaks

Your logging streak sits on Home next to today's calories — tap it to see your last 7 days.

Reminders

Optional meal and water reminders on your schedule — your first meal time, how many meals, how often.

Water your way

Pick your glass, can or bottle size, sync with Apple Health or not — or turn water tracking off.

Your units

Metric, US, UK or Australian: kilograms, pounds or stone, millilitres or fl oz, calories or kilojoules.

iCloud sync

Your log follows you to your other devices through your own private iCloud. Back up and restore anytime.

Share & export

Share a day, a week or a single meal as text. Export all your data, photos included.

Your diary lives on your iPhone.

Meals, photos, water and your profile are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud — we can't see them. Photos and descriptions are sent only to be analyzed, then discarded; nutrition labels are read on your iPhone. Connect an AI assistant and only a short summary of your recent meals is shared, never Apple Health data.
